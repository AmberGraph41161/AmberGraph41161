A list of other projects that I have worked on:
1. [keyboardMouse](https://github.com/AmberGraph41161/keyboardMouse): Daemon to control your mouse with your keyboard
1. [ncursesMinesweeper](https://github.com/AmberGraph41161/ncursesMinesweeper): play minesweeper in the terminal! (comes with a cool and flashy rainbow cursor)
1. [SFMLFlappyBird](https://github.com/AmberGraph41161/SFMLflappybird): a flappy bird clone where ballistic missiles are periodically shot towards the player
1. [SFMLTetris](https://github.com/AmberGraph41161/SFMLtetris): a simple tetris clone made with SFML3


I only really ever push projects that I am relatively proud of online, and tend
to shy away from posting other projects of mine.

However, if I had to list a couple of other projects that I have been working
on that are not listed here, they would be:
1. nclpmhk: abbreviation of "Ncurses LaunchPad-Mini Hot Key" which is a way to bind LaunchPad-Mini midi keys to actions on your pc
1. voiceAssistant: an experimentation projects with vosk-api and piper-tts libraries, mapping voice-commands to tts responses and actions
